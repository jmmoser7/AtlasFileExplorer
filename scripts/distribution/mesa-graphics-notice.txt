Slate includes the x64 OpenGL libraries from the Mesa 25.2.8 Windows MSVC build.

The Mesa source distribution includes its license notices.
https://archive.mesa3d.org/mesa-25.2.8.tar.xz

The Windows build scripts and exact binary distribution are available here.
https://github.com/pal1000/mesa-dist-win/tree/25.2.8
https://github.com/pal1000/mesa-dist-win/releases/tag/25.2.8

The llvmpipe CPU renderer includes LLVM 21.1.7. Its license and related
third-party notices are in LLVM-LICENSE.txt.
https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/tree/llvmorg-21.1.7
